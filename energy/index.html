Energy log

How much energy does my Claude use take?

I’ve been using Claude a lot recently for research and side projects, and I’m curious how much energy all those tokens add up to. To better understand my usage, this page estimates its energy footprint week by week using Claude Code’s token logs, with everyday comparisons to put the numbers in perspective.

How it's worked out

Claude Code records the tokens of every response on my computer. A script adds them up by day and model, and only those totals are published: no prompts, replies or project names. Tokens are weighted by Anthropic's list prices, as a stand-in for the computing work behind them, and scaled so that a typical chatbot answer (about 1,000 tokens in, 500 out) takes 0.3 Wh, the figure published estimates converge on. Nobody publishes per-token energy for Claude, so the range runs from three times lower to three times higher.

It counts Claude Code only (not chats on claude.ai or in the app), and only running the model: not training it, the hardware or my own computer. The code, the data and every assumption, with sources, are on GitHub.
