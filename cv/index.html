Research experience

  1. Sep 2024 – present

    Graduate Researcher, Pacific Ocean Neutrino Experiment (P-ONE)

    Simon Fraser University · Co-supervisors: Dr. Darren Grant & Dr. Matthias Danninger

    • Instrumentation, integration, testing and calibration of detector subsystems for a km³-scale deep-sea neutrino observatory.
    • Developing acoustic positioning and detector-geometry reconstruction methods for a dynamic marine environment.
    • Detector commissioning, quality assurance, and deployment preparation for P-ONE-1.
  2. Sep 2023 – Sep 2024

    Research Co-op, GRIFFIN Collaboration

    TRIUMF · UBC Science Co-op · Supervisor: Dr. Adam Garnsworthy

    • Developed ancillary scintillator and photomultiplier detector systems for coincidence tagging.
    • Detector operation, experimental shifts and data collection with radioactive ion beams.
    • Supported nuclear structure and reaction studies.
  3. May – Sep 2023

    Research Co-op, Theory Group

    TRIUMF · UBC Science Co-op · Supervisor: Dr. Jason Holt

    • IMSRG calculations of nuclear matrix elements relevant to neutrinoless double-beta decay.
  4. May – Sep 2019

    Machine Learning Research Co-op

    Laser Zentrum Hannover, Germany · UBC Engineering Co-op

    • Machine learning for process control in laser additive manufacturing and single-crystal turbine-blade repair.
